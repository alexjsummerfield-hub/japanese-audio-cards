## Filled patterns

- My name is Alex. The handout’s name blank is filled with Alex.
- I am not Australian. The negative pattern uses Australian, already introduced in lesson 1.
- Where is the station? The place blank is filled with station, used in the supplied travel material.
- Does this train go to Tokyo? Tokyo fills the destination blank; hear and learn Tōkyō here before practising.
- Is breakfast available? The item blank uses breakfast from the hotel lesson.
- Please show me this. The object blank uses kore, already introduced in this lesson.
- One of these, please. The item blank uses kore; ikko is introduced here from the handout’s one-item pattern.
- I lost my passport. The lost-item blank uses passport from the hotel lesson.

## Scope and editing

Repeated phrases are merged, including the two price questions. The emergency-number reference statement is not a speaking card. The request word kudasai is taught inside complete requests. Yoroshiku and onegai shimasu from the introductions sheet are combined as one standard introduction formula. The bag question is marked as something staff may say. Keyword meanings describe the phrase in context rather than claiming exact word-for-word equivalence. The list is a learning aid, not an automatic pronunciation assessment.
